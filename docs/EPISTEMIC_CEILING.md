# Epistemic ceiling

The conclusion limit is computed by `app.onehealth.evidence.ceiling` from the existing evidence assessment, not generated by an LLM. The UI displays its returned statements.

| Evidence | Strongest supported use |
|---|---|
| Citizen report/photo | Trigger environmental investigation; appearance cannot identify arsenic |
| Unverified laboratory report | Describe a submitted report; obtain independent verification |
| Verified sample | Describe that sample at its documented site/time |
| All six gates | Eligible for human review of exposure context |
| Complete evidence plus human clinical review | Consented, reviewed exposure context |
| Consent withdrawn | Retain historical audit; block clinical sharing/new exchange |

The gates are laboratory verification, active consent, drinking-water pathway, point-of-use sample, treatment/use context and temporal overlap. Removing any one recomputes eligibility on a copy and never changes stored evidence. Mapping approval and FHIR validation do not imply laboratory verification or permission to share clinical data.

No level permits disease diagnosis, individual disease probability, causality, historical dose inference from one sample or water-safety certification. Total arsenic is not silently treated as inorganic arsenic dose. Existing oncology authorization and twin scores remain independently computed from their own validated inputs.

Retest creates a new immutable sample, links `retest_of`/`successor_id`, and closes the original environmental Task. Verification, clinical review and consent/pathway attestation are not inherited. Comparison converts supported units to ug/L and requires matching analyte, matrix, site and exact-value qualifiers; it remains a measurement comparison requiring reviewer interpretation.
